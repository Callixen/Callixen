CREON — AI Labor Intelligence

Dashboard Labor Market Open Source Node.js

An open-source labor intelligence terminal mapping 341 US occupations against AI displacement risk, built on BLS OES 2024 employment and wage data. AI exposure scores are derived from a composite of Felten et al. (2023), Eloundou et al. / OpenAI (2023), and Goldman Sachs research — surfacing which roles are most vulnerable to automation and which are being created by it.

The dashboard renders five interactive visualisations: a D3 treemap of all 341 occupations sized by employment, a US choropleth of state-level displacement risk, a 22-sector heatmap, a wage-versus-exposure scatter plot, and a Fear Index gauge built from six survey datasets. WEF Future of Jobs 2025 data is embedded throughout — 170 million new roles forecast against 92 million displaced by 2030.

Backed by a Node.js relay server handling live market data (NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, BTC, ETH, SOL and more), real-time BLS macroeconomic series, and a newsdata.io research feed. Designed to accept any OpenClaw agent via a local relay — enabling direct conversational queries against the live data.
